# Introduce

Move from a checked candidate into the owner's actual workflow. Identify who uses
it, when, where input comes from, where output goes, who supports it and the manual
fallback. Show the real change to existing work; avoid an extra application people
must maintain without a benefit. For process changes, trial the new rule explicitly.

Before deployment/configuration, show destination, accounts, visibility, data,
cost and reversal, then obtain the applicable authority. Development permission
is not permission to publish, send or migrate production. Preserve a backup and
rehearse restore on a copy when data can be affected. Keep unconnected parts honest.

After authorized activation test the actual published/build identity and principal
workflow, not only a local fixture. Provide a usable open/run instruction, limits,
data/access map, contact/escalation and diagnosis/recovery/retire steps. Do not expose
localhost with a tunnel without permission. Do not claim a stopped preview is usable.

Evidence: the named version/environment and observed working action. Record trial
use separately from sustained adoption and benefit. Propose an observation period;
never invent future use or create timers without request.
