# Retire

Clarify whether the owner wants to pause, replace, transfer or permanently retire.
Map data, retention obligations, scheduled jobs, integrations, credentials, billing
and upstream/downstream users. A decision not to renew a tool does not delete data.

Prepare an export in a usable format, test that it opens, identify what must remain,
stop only owned jobs after permission and revoke the exact connections authorized.
Confirm subscription cancellation through the actual service rather than assuming
uninstall did it. Do not delete records, backups or other users' access without scope.

For replacement, verify the new workflow before retiring the old fallback when
appropriate. Preserve a minimal current record of what remains and where. Removing
Playbook itself must preserve the user's project and customized skills via the existing
connect/remove protocol; it is not the same action as retiring the created solution.

Deliver observed off-state, preserved/exported data, residual dependencies/costs,
retention/deletion decisions and how to reopen or continue. Preparation is not an
executed shutdown. External revocation and deletion require real receipts/inspection.
