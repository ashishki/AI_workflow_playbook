# Observe

Measure the real work after introduction against the recorded baseline under
comparable circumstances. Include preparation, AI waiting, checking, fixes and
coordination, not just the automated substep. Ask whether the owner actually used
it on repeated work and what was abandoned or done manually.

Keep technical correctness, actual use, process effect and Playbook's incremental
contribution separate. Log owner clarification, author orchestration and necessary
specialist help separately with reason and time. Zero help in a scripted test is
not a finding that novices need none. Costs include main/review/fix/eval, subscriptions
and people; unknown stays unknown and cached tokens are not counted twice.

Use ordinary project observations; the author's comparative Harness Lab is not
required on a user's machine. Do not start hidden tracking or contact people. A
week/month result can be entered only when actually observed. Causality, market
readiness and universal efficiency cannot follow from one positive case.

Deliver continue/change/stop recommendation, observed evidence and gaps. A worse
result is useful information, not a reason to manipulate metrics. Continue to
Change, Diagnose, Grow or Retire according to the actual need.
