# One working conversation, with continuity

Use this when beginning a work problem or returning to a solution. Do not turn
this into a questionnaire or another mandatory ledger. Reuse current project
state, owner examples and already settled decisions.

## Start from work, not an application

Ask for a recent concrete instance of the problem and how it is handled today.
Identify the next useful outcome, rules/exceptions that affect it, data authority,
and a baseline the owner can observe. Keep uncertain facts explicit. Ask only
what changes the next decision; do not demand architecture choices from the owner.

Compare a process change, configuration of existing tools and creation only when
those alternatives are plausible. AI is optional in the resulting solution.
Explain costs, ongoing attention and reversibility. A no-build decision needs a
reason, not a decorative success badge. A clear code task need not repeat discovery.

## Deliver linked behaviour

Retain the agreed outcome while progressing through the needed lifecycle routes.
After a component is ready, continue to the next dependency-ready step; do not
stop at a scaffold or ask "continue?" after every routine edit. Replan explicitly
when new business rules, permissions, risks or expenses change the decision.

For a UI, check purpose/content/interaction and visual coherence separately from
build success. Use existing frontend guidance and an appropriate design resource.
For integrations, distinguish local substitute, configured account and observed
external effect. Report actual gaps rather than stretching the word "done".

## Return or hand over

At a new session resolve the real checkout, local changes and applicable project
instructions before taking historical state as current. Verify relevant saved
references and snapshots; preserve other people's changes. Reuse the same record
of purpose, rules, data, integrations, costs, checks, problems and next step.

A new rule requires one new scenario and protection of affected old behaviour.
A transfer needs a new session/person to do meaningful work without the author's
chat. Written instructions alone do not close transfer acceptance. The optional
[state helper](state.md) can detect missing/stale local evidence; it does not
judge the truth of a report, provide backups or grant access.

## End in the owner's language

Give the outcome and a concrete way to open/use it, then what was actually checked,
what remains unavailable and the next meaningful decision. Do not claim a stopped
preview is open. Include location of data and ownership implications when changed.
Keep internal roles/logs accessible without requiring the owner to read them.
Observe later use only after it happens; do not schedule tracking without request.
